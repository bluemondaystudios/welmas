Meet the baker

Hi from Lerato the Baker

FreshlyBaked by Welmas began in 2018 in Ngwaritsi, Limpopo, with a simple idea: the cake at the centre of your celebration should taste as good as it looks — and it should be made by someone who cares.

What started with cakes for family and neighbours has grown into a full celebration kitchen: layered birthday cakes, tubs of melting moments and scones for big gatherings, burgers and milkshakes, platters, and complete kiddies and romantic setups.

Premium bakery. Made with love.

Always learning

Advanced training, real craft

Lerato completed a three-day Advanced Baking & Decorating course at Thatokie Baking Studio, working on tiered structures, doweling, fondant, sugar ruffles, stencilling and gold-leaf finishes.

Those skills now go into every Welmas cake — from a 6" birthday cake to a multi-tier centrepiece.
